# Product Vision

PhiOffice369 is a free, local-first, AI-assisted productivity suite built around a simple belief:

**People should be able to make useful documents, spreadsheets, decks, notes, diagrams, and project systems without being trapped by subscriptions, forced cloud accounts, or confusing enterprise software.**

The suite should feel friendly enough for a family laptop, capable enough for small businesses and creators, and structured enough for PHI369 Labs / Parallax artifact workflows.

## What makes it different

Most office suites treat files as isolated containers. PhiOffice369 treats files as connected artifacts.

A document can become a deck.  
A deck can become a handout.  
A spreadsheet can become a dashboard.  
A notebook can become a source map.  
A project folder can become a living workspace.

## Intended users

- Families
- Students and self-learners
- Creators
- Small businesses
- Community groups
- Builders and open-source teams
- PHI369 Labs / Parallax projects

## North star

A person should be able to open PhiOffice369, describe what they need, choose a template, work locally, use AI help transparently, and export something practical without feeling manipulated, locked in, or talked down to.
