# Trust Panel

PhiOffice369 should make AI help visible and honest.

## Core labels

- **Human-written** — written directly by the user.
- **AI-assisted** — edited, expanded, summarized, or generated with AI help.
- **Sourced** — backed by visible citations, files, or references.
- **Needs citation** — factual claim that should be supported before publishing.
- **Hypothesis** — plausible but unverified idea.
- **Symbolic** — metaphorical, mythic, artistic, or interface-layer meaning.
- **Private** — sensitive, personal, internal, or not for publication.
- **Verified** — checked against a trusted source or project record.

## Purpose

The trust panel is not meant to shame the user. It exists to protect clarity, authorship, privacy, and public trust.
